## The scale

Two things are being judged tonight, and each has to be a number before it is an opinion.

### Reaction: how long between a thing happening and the right bot moving

| event | first response | A | B | C | D |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| an enemy seen on our ground | the first member takes `rally` (defence) | < 5 s | < 15 s | < 60 s | slower |
| a guildmate's cry | the first `rescue` taken | < 5 s | < 15 s | < 60 s | slower |
| a war declared | the first company formed | < 30 s | < 2 min | < 10 min | slower |
| a funded order on the board | a maker takes it | < 1 min | < 5 min | < 20 min | slower |
| a bot dies | back on its feet | 60 s by design | | | |

The first two are measured by the shard from tonight (`BotFeud.ReactionMs`, `BotCry` → `BotRescuer`);
the rest are read off timestamps in the log until they are.
