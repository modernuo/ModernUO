Bot pathfinding, measured

Autonomous bots on a ModernUO shard walked by one tile search from where they stood, stopped by a clock of 60 to 150 ms. On 26.09.2026 that was replaced by a coarse chart (builds 262, 263) and then by tiered navigation in the engine (build 264). The charts and tables are read from the shard's logs; the design notes come from the project's decision log.

The three tiers

The design the owner supplied on 26.09.2026, and how far it was built that day. A tier hands the one below it something shorter to solve.

Longregions of 128 tiles
Regions of 8 × 8 clusters. An A* over the nodes on region borders gives the regions a route passes through; those and the ring round them are a corridor. Portals (teleporters, moongates) and a route cache belong here.
Build 265: regions and corridor, 1,792 regions on Felucca; not asked once on the live shard, where the configured Medium budget of 60,000 nodes was never exceeded. Portals, route cache: not yet.
a corridor of regions
Mediumclusters of 16 tiles
Hierarchical A* over gates between clusters: one entrance per pair of standable surfaces, so a bridge and the road under it stay apart; directed edges, since a drop is not a climb. Union-find components answer "unreachable" without a search.
Build 264: the whole of Felucca, 114,688 clusters, drawn on the game loop in 17.6 s of loop time. Build 268: read back from disk in 234 ms.
about one waypoint per cluster
Shorttiles
The bot's own tile A* from where it stands to the next waypoint, up to 24 tiles ahead, with everything that moves: doors, creatures, items. Still stopped by a clock, but now asked short questions.
BotPath, unchanged: 10/14 step costs, heights in bands of 20.
One step costs 100straight or diagonal: the game's step delay does not depend on direction.
A diagonal costs 1 moreonly to break ties towards straighter paths.
The estimate is 100 × Chebyshevthe larger of |dx| and |dy|, so it never overestimates and A* stays optimal on its graph.

One route, asked of the chart and of the graph

From the east bank of the river to The Lantern's hall door: 84 tiles in a straight line. Asked through the shard's console on build 262 (the chart) and build 264 (the graph). The old walker failed this walk three times that afternoon: "no way round it was found in 150ms".

Data: data/*.csv and data/key_numbers.json, regenerated from the shard's logs by python extract.py <logs folder>, which also refreshes the data embedded in this page. The write-up is in README.md.

Counting: summaries are cumulative since the world loaded; a session's age runs from its first log line. Search time per minute is time on the game loop's thread. Walker drops come from the walker's own lines: "gave up" is the bounded search giving up ("no way round it was found", "could not get one tile closer"); "proved" is a verdict about the ground ("there is no way from here", "nowhere there to stand", "shut in", "the ground has no way there"). Builds are not named in the logs; the marked sessions come from the project's decision log.
