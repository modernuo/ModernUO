Proving ground: bot strength measured by fights

A shard of autonomous bots (ModernUO, Ultima Online, Renaissance era) judged every bot's strength by a formula, Power = HitsMax × (average hit + Magery / 2). On 26.09.2026 it began to measure it instead: a copy of the bot, with its skills, stats, weapon and kit, fights a real creature in Green Acres, and the fight is read as R = share of the creature killed × HitsMax / net damage taken. R ≥ 1 means the bot beats that creature. This page shows every fight in the shard's logs and compares the measurement with the formula.

Over the day

Two moments split the day. At 03:43 (build 249) the rings were woken: until then the engine kept every creature on the ring asleep, because it runs a creature's mind only near a player's client, and bots have none. At 13:16 (build 259) the whole world round the bots was woken the same way.

Archers: weak, not broken

Archers lost to the orcish mage having dealt almost nothing, so from build 254 the first three bow fights after every boot were traced once a second. Each row below is one traced fight. The dots are shots (drops in the arrow count; the first shot of a fight is read from the ammunition the fight line reports at the start). In build 254 the first arrow waited 7 to 8 seconds; build 255 fixed it, and afterwards the first arrow flies at once.

The world sleeps where no player is

The door verb awake counts, round every bot, the creatures whose mind is on. For six minutes at 04:08 the dungeons round the bots were woken by hand.
